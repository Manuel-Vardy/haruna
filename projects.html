Impact in Tamale South

Projects for
Tamale South

The 24-Hour Economy Market: A Hub for Prosperity

Hon. Iddrisu, in collaboration with H.E. President John Dramani Mahama, recently broke ground on the Tamale 24-Hour Economy Market at Kukuo. This landmark project is more than a trading space; it is a flagship component of the NDC’s national economic transformation agenda.

  • Integrated Design: This is the largest market model under the 24-hour initiative, incorporating comprehensive support systems to boost local productivity.
  • Economic Impact: The facility is designed to support round-the-clock commerce, featuring 132 open sheds, 10 warehouses, cold storage for perishables, a lorry terminal, and a butcher shop.
  • Essential Services: To ensure the dignity and safety of traders, the market includes a six-bed clinic, a permanent Police and Fire Station, a crèche for working mothers, and a Women’s Bank to provide secure financial services for small-scale traders.
  • Strategic Goal: As the MP for Tamale South, Hon. Iddrisu championed this project to reposition local produce through value addition, driving industrialization and providing stable, dignified jobs for the youth and women of Tamale.

Northern Water Crisis Resolution

Recognizing that sustainable development is impossible without access to clean water, Hon. Iddrisu has made the resolution of the Tamale water crisis a top-tier priority.

  • Direct Advocacy: Having consistently pressured the government to declare Tamale a "water-scarce zone," he is now working directly within the current administration to secure the necessary funding and technical resources to overhaul the region’s aging water infrastructure.
  • A Holistic Commitment: During the recent sod-cutting ceremony, he reaffirmed that water provision is inextricably linked to the success of the 24-hour market. His office is coordinating with the Ghana Water Company to ensure that the new market and the surrounding communities have reliable, high-capacity supply lines.
  • Action-Oriented Governance: Beyond high-level policy, he continues to support the drilling of mechanized boreholes in the most vulnerable pockets of Tamale South, providing immediate relief while working on the permanent, large-scale engineering solutions required to solve the metropolis's perennial shortage.
Education Ministry

Minister for
Education

As Minister for Education, Hon. Haruna Iddrisu's focus has been on continuity, infrastructure expansion, and equitable access to education.

Key Projects & Initiatives

Completion of Ongoing and Abandoned Projects

A major policy direction has been the commitment to complete all inherited and stalled educational infrastructure projects nationwide.

  • Ensures efficient use of public resources
  • Accelerates delivery of school infrastructure
  • Promotes continuity in governance

Expansion of School Infrastructure

  • Construction and commissioning of school facilities (e.g., dormitories, classrooms)
  • Improvement in teaching and learning environments
  • Support for underserved and rural communities
Secondary school (SHS) expansion & upgrades
  • 30 Community Day SHSs (E-Blocks) to be completed
  • 10 schools upgraded from Category B → A
  • 30 schools upgraded from Category C → B
  • Additional upgrades to ~30+ SHSs (Category B & C) under a $200M–$250M World Bank facility
Basic education infrastructure (national programme)
  • 600 new classroom blocks planned for 2026, broken down into:
  • 200 KG blocks
  • 200 Primary blocks
  • 200 JHS blocks
Funding commitments (not project counts)
  • GH₵1.1 billion allocated for SHS infrastructure expansion under GSLIP
  • GH₵300 million requested for Category A SHSs expansion
  • Broader commitment of GH₵500 million – GH₵1 billion for nationwide infrastructure
Other individual projects mentioned
  • Construction/commissioning of:
  • A girls' dormitory (Bimbilla)
  • A planned TVET Centre of Excellence (Salaga)
Communication Ministry

Minister for
Communication

As Minister for Communication, Hon. Haruna Iddrisu led major reforms that strengthened Ghana's ICT ecosystem and expanded digital access nationwide.

Key Projects & Initiatives

Mobile Number Portability (MNP)

One of the most impactful reforms under his tenure was the introduction of Mobile Number Portability, allowing telecom users to switch networks without changing their phone numbers.

  • Increased competition among telecom operators
  • Improved service quality and pricing
  • Empowered consumers with choice

SIM Card Registration Exercise

To enhance national security and regulatory oversight, his ministry implemented the nationwide SIM card registration policy.

  • Improved identification of mobile users
  • Reduced fraud and anonymous communications
  • Strengthened national security systems

Data Protection Framework

He championed the passage and implementation of Ghana's Data Protection regime, ensuring that personal data of citizens is safeguarded.

  • Established legal backing for privacy protection
  • Increased trust in digital services
  • Aligned Ghana with global ICT standards

National Data Centre Project

Under his leadership, the government invested in the National Data Centre, a critical infrastructure for digital governance.

  • Centralized data storage for government institutions
  • Improved efficiency in public service delivery
  • Strengthened Ghana's digital infrastructure

Expansion of ICT Access & Policy Leadership

  • Participation in global ICT platforms such as ITU and WSIS
  • Promotion of ICT development policies across sectors
  • Strengthening institutions like GIFEC to expand rural connectivity

Impact: His tenure laid the foundation for Ghana's modern telecom and digital governance systems, making ICT a key driver of economic growth.

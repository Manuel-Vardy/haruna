Dedicated Public Servant & Visionary Leader

Haruna Iddrisu is a distinguished Ghanaian statesman, lawyer, and parliamentarian known for his unwavering commitment to democratic governance and national development. Serving multiple terms as Member of Parliament for Tamale South, he has earned a reputation as a principled leader and effective legislator.

Who He Is

A Life of Service

A Legacy of
Dedicated Service

Statesman Hon. Haruna Iddrisu is a distinguished Ghanaian statesman, legal practitioner, and dedicated public servant whose career is defined by eloquence, pragmatic leadership, and a steadfast commitment to the development of Ghana. Currently serving as the Minister for Education and the Member of Parliament for Tamale South, he has spent over two decades shaping national policy and advocating for the rights and welfare of the Ghanaian people.

Born on September 8, 1970, in Tamale, Hon. Iddrisu’s passion for service began early, notably during his tenure as the President of the National Union of Ghana Students (NUGS). Since entering Parliament in 2005, he has earned a reputation as a brilliant orator and a consensus builder, capable of navigating complex national challenges with logic, coherence, and integrity.

Personal Life & Values

While Hon. Haruna Iddrisu’s public life is marked by intense parliamentary and ministerial activity, he maintains a balanced personal life. He is married to Mrs. Mary Safua, and together they are dedicated to raising their family with the same values of integrity, education, and hard work that define his public career. Despite the public nature of his work, he remains a grounded figure who values his privacy and finds strength in his faith and the support of his family.

Ministerial Leadership

Education
Reform

Currently spearheading transformative initiatives as Minister for Education, including the “No-Fees-Stress” digital platform to streamline tertiary education applications and financial aid, and the establishment of a GHS 50 million National Research Fund to boost the knowledge economy.

Ministerial Leadership

Employment &
Labour Relations

During his tenure, he played a pivotal role in strengthening industrial relations, notably chairing the Technical Cooperation of the 323rd Governing Body Session of the International Labour Organisation (ILO).

Ministerial Leadership

Modernizing
Communications

Championed the modernization of Ghana’s telecommunications sector by introducing Mobile Number Portability, mandatory SIM card registration, and implementing the Data Protection Law to safeguard citizen privacy.

Parliamentary Service & Representation

Constituency Development

A dedicated representative for the people of Tamale South, having won the seat in six consecutive parliamentary elections. He is noted for his unique record of running unopposed in party primaries four consecutive times, reflecting deep constituent trust.

Legislative Excellence

Served as the Minority Leader (2017–2023), recognized for his strategic leadership, disciplined approach to parliamentary business, and effective advocacy. A master tactician, he played a critical role in the successful election of Rt. Hon. Alban Bagbin as Speaker in 2021, fostering a more deliberative environment.

Institutional Governance

Served on various high-level parliamentary committees, including the Appointments, Business, and Special Budget committees, contributing to the oversight and accountability of public institutions.

Strategic Support (2024)

Instrumental in crafting the narrative for H.E. John Dramani Mahama’s 2024 campaign, synthesizing complex issues into clear communication that solidified the party’s platform and broadened its appeal.

Youth & Sports Empowerment

Transformative force in sports development through ownership of Karela United FC and Steadfast FC, providing professional opportunities for young footballers transitioning from local pitches.

Scholarships

Educational
Empowerment

Haruna Iddrisu, the Member of Parliament for Tamale South and current Minister for Education, has indeed been associated with supporting students through scholarships and educational assistance over the years.

Fields of Support

His scholarship support has typically covered multiple fields, including:

  • Medicine and health-related programs
  • Engineering and technical disciplines
  • Education and teacher training
  • ICT and communication studies
  • Business and social sciences

Modes of Intervention

These interventions are often done through:

  • Personal initiatives and constituency support schemes
  • Partnerships with organizations and donor bodies

The broader goal behind these efforts is to expand access to higher education, especially for students from less privileged backgrounds, particularly within the Northern Region and beyond.

Vision

Supporting the
Next Generation

Haruna Iddrisu, Member of Parliament for Tamale South and Ghana's Minister for Education, has consistently demonstrated a strong commitment to empowering young people through education. Over the years, he has supported numerous students by providing scholarships and educational assistance across a wide range of academic disciplines.

Recognizing education as a powerful tool for national development, his scholarship initiatives have benefited students pursuing programs in:

  • Medicine and health sciences
  • Engineering and applied sciences
  • Education and teacher training
  • Information and Communication Technology (ICT)
  • Business administration and the social sciences

These interventions are designed not only to ease the financial burden on students and their families but also to nurture talent and build a skilled workforce for Ghana's future.

Through a combination of personal initiatives, constituency-based support schemes, and collaborations with institutions and development partners, Hon. Haruna Iddrisu has helped many young people access higher education opportunities both locally and internationally.

His efforts have particularly impacted students from underserved communities, especially within the Northern Region, ensuring that financial constraints do not limit academic potential.

Economic Bridge

The Bridge
Initiative

"Ghana NEEDS A BRIDGE"

The Bridge Programme is a nationally branded yet independently governed initiative that bridges Ghana's graduates to industry opportunity and entrepreneurship capital which operates through three interlocking pillars.

As Ghana stands at a critical inflection point. Each year, thousands of graduates emerge from universities, polytechnics, TVET institutions, and training programmes into an economy that is yet to build adequate pathways to absorb talents. Youth unemployment for ages 15 to 35 stands at 22.5%, rising to 32% for ages 15 to 24. Nearly 68% of employed youth are in vulnerable, low paying jobs offering no long-term security. The gap between education and employment has become one of the defining challenges of this generation.

My Bridge initiative Programme is a national initiative designed to close that gap. It is built on three interlocking pillars: a selective, prestigious fellowship programme that connects high-potential graduates to industry; a broad-access training and placement platform that serves youth across all 16 regions; and an endowment fund that converts graduate talent into job-creating businesses.
